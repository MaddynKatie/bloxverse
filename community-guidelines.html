Community Guidelines

Keeping BloxVerse safe, fun, and welcoming for everyone.

BloxVerse is a creative platform built for players and developers of all ages. These guidelines exist to make sure everyone — regardless of background — can enjoy the experience. We expect all users to treat each other with respect and follow these rules at all times.

🤝

Be Respectful

Treat every player with kindness. Disagreements are normal — harassment is not.

🎮

Play Fair

Don't use exploits, cheats, or hacks. Win through skill, not shortcuts.

🔒

Protect Privacy

Never share personal information — yours or others'. Don't ask for it either.

🛡️

Keep It Safe

Content and games must be appropriate for a general audience. No adult content.

🚫

Zero Tolerance

Hate speech, threats, and targeted harassment result in immediate permanent bans.

✅

Report Issues

See something wrong? Use our Support Form to report it. Don't retaliate yourself.

What's Not Allowed

  • Harassment & Bullying — repeatedly targeting a player to make them feel unwelcome, threatened, or humiliated.
  • Hate Speech — content that degrades individuals based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.
  • Exploiting & Cheating — using unauthorized scripts, programs, or game bugs to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Impersonation — pretending to be a BloxVerse moderator, staff member, or another user to deceive others.
  • Scamming — deceiving others out of BUX, items, or any in-game resource.
  • Inappropriate Content in Games — publishing games that contain gore, sexual content, or content that glorifies self-harm.
  • Real-Money Trading — attempting to sell or buy BUX or in-game items for real currency.
  • Account Sharing & Ban Evasion — sharing accounts with others or creating new accounts to get around a ban.
  • Spam & Advertising — repeatedly posting identical messages or promoting external services/links.

Content Creation Rules

When building and publishing games or assets on BloxVerse, you must:

  • Ensure your game is appropriate for users ages 13 and up.
  • Only use assets you own or have permission to use.
  • Not hide prohibited content within scripts or models.
  • Not use your game to collect personal data from players.
  • Keep game descriptions and thumbnails accurate and appropriate.

Enforcement Actions

Violations are handled on a case-by-case basis. The following table outlines typical enforcement for common violations:

Violation Severity Typical Action
Minor spam / off-topic messages Low Warning
Mild harassment Medium 1–7 day ban
Exploiting / cheating Medium 7–30 day ban
Severe harassment / threats High Permanent ban
Hate speech High Permanent ban
Inappropriate game content High Game removal + ban
Ban evasion (new account) High All accounts permanently banned

Appealing a Decision

If you believe your account was actioned unfairly, you may submit an appeal through our Support Form. Select "Ban Appeal" as the category. Appeals are reviewed by a senior moderator within 5 business days. Appeal decisions are final.

To help your appeal, provide as much context as possible about what happened and why you believe the action was incorrect.

Reporting a Violation

If you witness a violation of these guidelines, please submit a report using our Support Form and select "Report a Player". Include the player's username and a description of what occurred. We review all reports and take appropriate action.
